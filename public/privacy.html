PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

How we collect, use and protect the personal information you share with us.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Riverside Racing Syndicate (“Riverside”, “we”, “us”). If you have any questions about this policy or your personal information, please contact us through the enquiry form on our contact page.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details: your full name, email address, phone number and any message you choose to include when you send an enquiry.
  • Technical information: when you visit the website, our hosting provider may process basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver and secure the site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and explain the syndicate, the horses and the next steps.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information. We will not add you to a mailing list or use your details for marketing unless you have asked us to.

Who we share it with

Enquiries are passed through a form-handling service that processes them on our behalf so that we can receive and reply to them. We may also disclose information where required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to respond to you and to meet any legal record-keeping obligations. After that, they are deleted.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you. Send your request through the enquiry form on our contact page and we will respond.

Racing information

Nothing on this website is financial advice or a guarantee of returns. Racing outcomes are uncertain. Please read the relevant syndicate agreement carefully before making any decision.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
