FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about the journey?

We’re here to help you get started.

Talk to us
What is a racing syndicate?

A racing syndicate brings people together around a shared racehorse experience. The ownership structure, entitlements, responsibilities and costs depend on the individual agreement.

Do I need to have racing experience?

You’re welcome to enquire whether you’re new to racing or have followed the sport for years.

When will the horse profiles be available?

Our horse profiles are coming soon. Enquire to register your interest; details such as the horse, trainer and opportunity will be shared when confirmed.

What costs and terms should I consider?

Share structures, initial costs and any ongoing contributions depend on the opportunity. Ask for the full details and review the syndicate agreement before making a decision.

Will I receive updates or be able to attend race days?

Updates, stable visits and race-day access can vary. Ask us what may be available for a particular opportunity, including any conditions or limitations.

Are financial returns guaranteed?

No. Racing outcomes and financial returns are uncertain, and costs may exceed any proceeds. Ask how prize money, expenses and any sale proceeds are treated under the relevant agreement.

What about horse welfare and retirement?

Horse welfare matters throughout a horse’s racing journey and beyond. Ask about the care, retirement and aftercare arrangements for any opportunity you are considering.

How do I find out more?

Send us your name, email and phone number. We can discuss your interest, explain available information and help you understand the next steps.
